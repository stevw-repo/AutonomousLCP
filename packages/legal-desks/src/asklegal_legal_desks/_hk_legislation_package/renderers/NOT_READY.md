# Renderer limitations

Package `0.41.0` freezes an executable source-neutral renderer for supported
textual bilingual locations and recursive serving partitioning at declared
official bilingual child boundaries. Partitioning measures the final text and
complete six-field metadata independently, includes actual part labels and
required repeated context, proves exact primary source-unit coverage, and never
uses arbitrary cuts. Twenty-two byte-locked cases cover every ADR 0040
condition at the source-neutral contract boundary, including statutory-note
attachment, complete table-row splitting with repeated bilingual header paths,
and the explicit effect-free ADR 0005 consequence when a new Official Version
cannot be partitioned safely.

The package does not implement authentic HKeL XML/XSD parsing, official-copy or
PDF reconciliation, Schedules, Forms, and asset
rendering, legislation-specific traceability entries, a real admitted tokenizer
profile, or Search Record construction. The canonical Plan executor refuses
unsupported structures and
produces only provisional trees and alignment. All three legislation scopes
therefore remain `NOT_READY`.
