# Freeze the initial HKEX Regulatory conformance catalogue

The audited conceptual catalogue in
[`HONG_KONG_REGULATORY_CONFORMANCE_CATALOGUE.md`](../design/HONG_KONG_REGULATORY_CONFORMANCE_CATALOGUE.md)
is the accepted initial Hong Kong Regulatory Materials conformance universe.
It freezes **284 permanent direct case IDs**, **284 matching primary coverage-
cell IDs**, and **57 permanent high-risk pair IDs** with their exact normative
scenarios, results, primary checkpoints, and pair membership.

The count follows direct coverage of the accepted legal and technical branches.
It was not chosen as a target, cap, sample size, model score, or Cartesian
product of independent facts.

## Frozen checkpoint counts

| Primary checkpoint | Direct cases and primary cells |
|---|---:|
| Source Fact Authority, membership, ownership, and language evidence | 62 |
| Applicability-branch effective state and transition | 51 |
| Record boundary, governing dependency, and cross-reference decision | 30 |
| Canonical English rendering, tables, fees, and Forms | 35 |
| Exact-limit measurement and official-structure partitioning | 24 |
| English source-unit coverage and per-board readiness | 20 |
| Search Record identity, lineage, and traceability | 24 |
| Package integrity, reproducibility, and forbidden effects | 38 |
| **Total** | **284** |

The evidence-to-decision layer contains 143 cases. The decision-to-artifact
deterministic layer contains 141 cases. One layer cannot compensate for a
failure in the other.

## Frozen identity and completeness

Each row is the direct primary case for one matching primary coverage cell.
Every case and cell ID is permanent, opaque, and non-answer-bearing. The IDs
identify only the checkpoint and ordinal; they do not encode the source, board,
state, expected result, success, failure, pair role, or critical-error status.
They never enter a proposal component's ordinary evidence packet.

All 57 pairs contain exactly one declared positive and one declared near-miss
member. A case participates in several pairs only where its one unchanged
scenario directly proves each controlled distinction. The executable coverage
matrix must enumerate every role explicitly.

Exact manifests and catalogues enumerate all case, coverage-cell, pair,
artifact, contract, input, reference, and expected-output identities.
Directory discovery, globs, ranges, filename conventions, aggregate counts,
broad tags, secondary coverage, or an average score cannot substitute for one
missing direct case, cell, branch, or pair member.

## Evidence-to-decision coverage

The 143 decision cases freeze direct results for:

- all five ordinary Registered Source roles, their permitted Fact Authorities,
  forbidden overreach, complete union, freshness, no-change, bounded failures,
  shared gaps, optional evidence, and approval inference;
- every accepted included, evidence-only, excluded, and unresolved membership
  family; Main Board and GEM ownership; shared artifacts; duplicates, orphans,
  double ownership, wrong-board assignment; and required English versus
  optional Chinese evidence;
- every applicability-branch state, component summary, fixed-date and time-zone
  boundary, current-product reconciliation, external trigger, all-of and any-of
  condition, cohort transition, overlap, replacement, supersession,
  withdrawal, unknown result, and ADR 0005 serving choice; and
- complete official record units, definitions, lists, governing dependencies,
  notes, appendices, Practice Notes, cross-references, cross-board targets, and
  safe or unsafe child boundaries.

The Hong Kong Regulatory Materials Legal Desk owns the accepted structured
reference decisions under the exact Source Rulebook. This freeze does not
decide whether a future proposal for any one semantic fact is deterministic,
generative-LLM-assisted, or human-produced.

## Decision-to-artifact coverage

The 141 deterministic cases freeze exact consequences for:

- canonical English context blocks, source fidelity, controlled Regulatory
  authority notes, tables, Fees Rules, Regulatory Forms, neutral controls, and
  prohibited Chinese serving paths;
- exact token and metadata-byte measurement, real final part labels, recursive
  official-structure partitioning, minimum-part and earliest-full selection,
  arbitrary-cut rejection, indivisible overlong material, and fixed-metadata
  failure;
- complete English source-unit ownership, dependency accounting, non-serving
  outcomes, source order, board isolation, Coverage Gaps, and serving readiness;
- immutable Search Record reuse, reselection, successors, partition and note
  changes, presentation-only reuse, traceability-only revisions, layered
  identity, lineage, and lookup validation; and
- strict packages, paths, hashes, media types, permissions, deliberate absence,
  unreadable inputs, exact and zero outputs, non-applicability, pair and matrix
  completeness, answer non-leakage, immutable correction, reproducibility,
  hostile source text, attestation binding, and forbidden external effects.

Every deterministic expected artifact role remains exactly `EXACT`, `NONE`, or
`NOT_APPLICABLE`. Missing output cannot pass as a zero result.

## Critical errors and pass rule

No aggregate score compensates for a designated critical error, including:

- guidance or another excluded item entering the rule corpus;
- wrong-board or ambiguous double ownership;
- future or conditional wording served as current without exact effect and
  current-product reconciliation;
- reconstruction, silent correction, or authority-note repair of missing,
  conflicting, or unknown law;
- omitted or flattened transitions and guessed cohort exhaustion;
- Chinese substitution, fabricated source text, Chinese duplicate records, or
  ignored English-defect signals;
- detached qualifications, table labels, fee bases, Form obligations, or
  governing dependencies;
- arbitrary splitting, truncation, incomplete primary source-unit coverage, or
  false serving readiness;
- mutable identities, cyclic lineage, or mismatched traceability;
- answer leakage, non-reproducible exact output, hostile-source influence; or
- any forbidden source, provider, network, credential, Azure, Pinecone, backup,
  routing, promotion, production-store, undeclared-file, or live-state effect.

Every required case and pair member must pass. Critical categories remain
separate from later semantic retrieval scores.

## Immutable expansion and correction

A new legal or technical requirement adds new case, coverage-cell, or pair IDs
without renumbering existing IDs. Correcting a frozen scenario, input,
reference result, required artifact, assertion, coverage binding, or pair
relationship requires a new versioned catalogue, explicit correction or
supersession mapping, impact declaration, preserved former package and result,
and revalidation of every affected rulebook, task, contract, and build.

The exact group counts and total describe this initial catalogue version. They
do not prevent evidence-backed expansion.

## What remains outside this freeze

This decision does not select or authorize:

- executable JSON Schemas, manifests, fixture bytes, canonical grammar tokens,
  stable rule or reason codes, or validator code;
- exact source artifacts, live endpoint registrations, current component rows,
  or operational cutoff evidence;
- generative-LLM task allocation, task contracts, model, prompt, settings,
  evaluation evidence, thresholds, cost, or retention values;
- an embedding model, token or metadata-byte ceiling, multilingual retrieval
  workflow, downstream-answer evaluator, or admission thresholds;
- implementation, source acquisition, evaluation execution, corpus
  construction, release publication, Pinecone or Azure access, promotion,
  deployment, commit, push, or any remote action.

## Consequences and authorization

Every future executable `hk-regulatory` suite and Regulatory Rulebook
Conformance Attestation must implement and pass this exact frozen catalogue.
It cannot omit, combine, weaken, dynamically discover, or mark a required case
inapplicable merely to fit cost, performance, implementation, or model limits.

This decision authorizes documentation only. It freezes conceptual design
requirements and grants no implementation or operational capability.

ADR 0076 later settles the high-level change-gated two-pass Regulatory
allocation without changing these frozen cases or enabling a model call. Exact
task contracts, providers, models, prompts, settings, evaluations, thresholds,
costs, and retention values remain outside this freeze.
