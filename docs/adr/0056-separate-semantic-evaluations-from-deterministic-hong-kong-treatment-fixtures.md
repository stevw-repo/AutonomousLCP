# Separate semantic evaluations from deterministic Hong Kong treatment fixtures

The Hong Kong later-treatment contract uses two linked but non-substitutable
conformance suites. One evaluates whether the LLM understands varied judgment
language. The other proves that accepted structured facts produce exact legal,
record, release, and serving consequences.

A model answer can be semantically correct without reproducing one preferred
prose explanation. Conversely, plausible model prose does not prove exact
authority-note bytes, identity, lineage, selection, release arithmetic, or
promotion eligibility. Combining both questions into one end-to-end score
would hide which trust boundary failed.

## Suite A — semantic model evaluations

The semantic suite gives the admitted model task complete synthetic evidence or
an exact permitted and preserved real judgment evaluation artifact. It tests
whether the model proposes the correct:

- treating passages and necessary context;
- earlier judgment and exact Case Proposition or bounded unresolved set;
- treatment class and expression mode;
- whole, partial, issue-specific, fact-specific, procedural, or unresolved
  scope;
- materiality, opinion attribution, operative status, and uncertainty; and
- structured evidence references required for deterministic validation.

The expected answer is schema-bound. The catalogue may permit several
explicitly enumerated structured answers when the difference is legally
immaterial, but it does not grade free-form prose similarity. Invented evidence,
missed mandatory treatment, wrong proposition mapping, wrong operative opinion,
or unsupported certainty is a failure even when the surrounding explanation
sounds plausible.

Synthetic examples isolate boundaries and adversarial near-misses. A sealed
representative real-judgment evaluation set tests authentic Hong Kong judicial
language, structure, length, and variation. Real judgment artifacts, model
outputs containing operational data, and evaluation results remain outside Git;
Git may contain small synthetic fixtures, contracts, catalogue definitions, and
non-sensitive fingerprints.

## Suite B — deterministic contract fixtures

The deterministic suite begins from a frozen candidate proposal or accepted
Legal Desk decision. It does not ask an LLM to rediscover the answer. Every
fixture requires canonical exact outputs for all applicable artifacts:

- validation result and stable reason codes;
- accepted Legal Desk decision and ordered Rule Trace;
- treatment-graph delta and treatment-screening accounting;
- controlled `metadata.authority_note` and rendered-note fingerprint;
- Search Record reuse, allocation, forward lineage, or exact former-record
  reselection;
- Search Record Selection Event;
- embedding reuse, generation, or omission decision;
- Corpus Release and Desired-State Inventory diff and arithmetic;
- update outcome, human-review route, and operational-pause state; and
- Promotion Manifest eligibility and absence of any direct live-index action.

Each fixture contains one strict manifest, minimal synthetic earlier and
treating judgments, exact opinion and paragraph structure, court and hierarchy
facts, finality, cutoff, identities, predecessor records, prior treatment graph,
and pinned rulebook and contract fingerprints. It also contains exact expected
artifacts or exact rejection and no-side-effect assertions. A fixture proves
contract behavior only; it proves no fact about real law.

## Coverage-driven catalogue

The catalogue has no arbitrary fixed total. Completeness is measured against a
frozen coverage matrix. Every legal-treatment class, authority and opinion
boundary, permitted expression mode, proposition-scope result, renderer-budget
branch, immutable-record transition, uncertainty path, correction and later-
status change, update outcome, model safety failure, and review-routing branch
must have direct coverage.

High-risk boundaries require paired near-misses. The catalogue must, at
minimum, distinguish:

- bare citation from material explanation, application, following, or approval;
- factual distinction from a material limiting distinction;
- doubt, criticism, disapproval, refusal to follow, and conclusive overruling;
- operative majority treatment from dissent, unadopted concurrence,
  non-controlling plurality, foreign-court discussion, and lower-court
  statements without overruling power;
- express meaning from permitted necessary reasoning and ambiguity;
- exact, partial, bounded-unknown, and unbounded-unknown proposition mapping;
- warning-first rendering, support and neutral context, equivalent-event
  consolidation, changed references, optional overflow, and mandatory-warning
  overflow;
- same-record reuse, forward successor, exact former-record reselection, full
  retirement, supported partial-retirement successors, no honest narrower
  proposition, and traceability-only change;
- corrected reasons, removed treatment passages, reversal, supersession, and
  evidence-backed reinstatement;
- no-proposition treating judgments, unmatched citations, HKLII-only leads,
  missing originating text, and treatment Coverage Gaps;
- complete-judgment fit, opinion-aware segmentation, complete coverage ledgers,
  forbidden silent truncation, invented evidence, wrong identity, and
  impossible hierarchy claims;
- ordinary automatic acceptance and reporting, uncertainty review, narrow
  exceptional-change review, and a separate operational anomaly pause; and
- supported no change, accounting-only change, serving change, blocked,
  quarantined, post-cutoff deferral, and stale-predecessor rejection.

No required row may be silently skipped, weakened, replaced by a broad aggregate
score, or marked inapplicable without a versioned coverage decision and impact
declaration.

## Acceptance rule

Every deterministic fixture must pass exactly, including canonical bytes,
stable rule and reason IDs, inventory arithmetic, and forbidden-side-effect
assertions.

Each admitted model, prompt, schema, or material task change must pass the
pinned semantic evaluation contract. That contract separately defines
aggregate thresholds, class- and boundary-specific thresholds, repeated-run
rules where applicable, and critical errors. The accepted evaluation set has
zero tolerance for designated critical errors such as fabricated evidence,
false conclusive overruling, operative-opinion inversion, or confident
resolution of a required unresolved scope.

Zero tolerance is an admission rule for the frozen evaluation set. It is not a
claim that a probabilistic model can never make such an error on unseen real
judgments. Runtime evidence validation, Quarantine, review routing, monitoring,
and revalidation remain mandatory.

The semantic suite cannot prove deterministic serving correctness. The
deterministic suite cannot prove that the model understands authentic varied
judgment language. Both suites and every applicable required row must pass
before a Hong Kong later-treatment model task and its downstream contract can
be admitted together.

## Change and evidence rules

Every catalogue, fixture, expected artifact, evaluation-set fingerprint,
annotation policy, accepted-answer set, model contract, threshold, and result is
versioned and immutable once used. A material change creates a new version and
impact declaration; it never silently rewrites a past attestation.

Human-approved expected semantic answers record the supporting passages,
accepted structured result, allowable variants, annotator or Legal Desk role,
adjudication state, and fingerprints. Chain-of-thought is neither required nor
stored as an expected answer.

## Consequences

ADR 0057 now fixes the stable namespaces, non-leaking strict package mechanics,
catalogues, common and suite-specific manifest requirements, expected-artifact
inventory, and coverage-matrix semantics. The next design task is the exact
initial coverage-cell and case table. Exact model choice, prompt, numerical
thresholds, real-judgment set composition, retry policy, and provider settings
remain later task-admission values and are not invented by this ADR.

This decision authorizes documentation only. It does not authorize
implementation, real-judgment acquisition, LLM or embedding-provider calls,
release publication, Pinecone mutation, promotion, or deployment.
